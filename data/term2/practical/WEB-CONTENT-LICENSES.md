## ECGpedia (ECG 01–20)

## User/course material

ECG 21 and 22 are unchanged user-provided assignment screenshots. The other station images are existing user-provided course references; their original rights remain with the respective owners. No open licence is asserted over these course images. The aVR assignment's machine estimate (about −40°) is distinguished from the idealised −60° answer implied by the stated equiphasic premise. Patient identifiers were not found in the newly selected figures. The axis screenshot's machine measurements are concealed during questions to avoid answer leakage.

## Question review and scope

The 320-question baseline was reassessed for source agreement, calibration, answer logic and distractor relevance. `quality-review.mjs` records exactly which questions were revised. Correct answer positions and original question IDs were retained to avoid breaking saved progress; prior attempts are not a substitute for revisiting revised content. This authoring audit is not independent clinician review.

The new 31 visual cases contain 124 individually authored questions, with four different tasks per case. Course-transfer cases apply the local pack; extension cases are clearly marked as additional learning, not confirmed exam scope. Missing scales, low-resolution scans, unkeyed local examples, and method-specific reference ranges remain explicit limitations.
