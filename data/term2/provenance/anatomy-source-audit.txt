All question facts were checked against the local Gray's Anatomy for Students, 3rd edition, clean PDF. Its extracted text is font-encoded gibberish, so the relevant sections were rendered and read using local OCR with visual checks of source pages and figures. No transcript text was used. The PDF's physical page index is twelve greater than its printed Arabic page number; both locators are recorded in every question.

011-018  Pharynx (8): chapter 8, Pharynx, printed pp1040-1052. Includes boundaries, tonsils, auditory tube, constrictor gap, stylopharyngeus, soft-palate closure and gag afferent.
019-030  Larynx (12): chapter 8, Larynx, printed pp1055-1069. Includes vocal-fold movement, recurrent/internal/external laryngeal nerves, cricothyroid mechanics, intrinsic ligaments, arytenoid processes and the anatomical cricothyroid interval.
031-037  Trachea, bronchi and segments (7): chapter 3, Pleural cavities/Lungs, printed pp169,172-173,178. Includes bifurcation, tracheal wall, aspiration, carina, lobar branching and segmental bronchovascular organization.

The source decks do not state the original illustration editions; that uncertainty is explicitly preserved in source.edition. The underlying figures were cross-checked with relevant Gray's 3e material, but the deck illustration itself is not mislabeled as a specific edition.
