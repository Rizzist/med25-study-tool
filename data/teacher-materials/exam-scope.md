## July 25 - Tissue Development & Function

### Active Histology scope

- Histological methods, preparation, microscopy and stains.
- Cell structure: membrane, signaling, organelles, cytoskeleton, nucleus, cell cycle and cell death.
- Epithelial, connective and adipose tissue.
- Cartilage, bone, ossification and joints.
- Blood and hemopoiesis.
- Muscle and nervous tissue.
- Skin.
- **Male and female reproductive-system Histology**, retained because it directly reinforces the Embryology course.

The ordinary text banks for circulatory/lymphoid, digestive and associated glands, respiratory, urinary, endocrine, eye/ear and special-sense Histology are excluded from the July 25 default. Their practical/image spotters remain active as an explicit exception because practical-derived theory and image analysis are examinable. All excluded text questions remain preserved in the master bank.

### Active Embryology scope

- Langman-aligned general embryology/current lectures: molecular regulation and signaling, gametogenesis, fertilization, first and second weeks, gastrulation and the third week, folding and weeks 3-8, fetal period, placenta and fetal membranes.
- Congenital malformations, teratogenic timing and prenatal screening/diagnosis.
- Teacher image material on gametogenesis, meiosis and chromosomal recognition remains active because image analysis is examinable.

The ordinary text banks for later system development and system-specific cardiovascular development remain excluded from the July 25 default. Teacher-derived image-recognition questions remain active as the practical/image exception.

### Active Physiology scope

- Guyton Chapters 1-5: homeostasis, cell physiology, genetic control of cellular function, membrane transport, resting membrane potential and action potentials.
- Skeletal-muscle contraction and excitation.
- Excitation-contraction coupling.
- Neuromuscular-junction transmission.
- Smooth-muscle contraction.
- General chemical synaptic transmission and neurotransmitters.

### Practical-derived theory and images

Practical-derived material is **not excluded**. Stains, methods, microscopy, slide recognition and embryo diagrams remain inside the July 25 mixed bank because their theory and image analysis can be tested. A separate **Practical + spotters** collection exposes the same material for targeted drilling; it is a study view, not a claim that a separate practical exam occurs on July 25.

## July 29 - Cell & Molecules

### Active Biochemistry scope

- Foundations, ATP, functional groups and chemical bonds.
- Water, pH, buffers, Henderson-Hasselbalch and titration.
- Mono- and disaccharide structure, glycoconjugates, lipids/lipoproteins, amino acids, proteins and hemoglobin.
- Enzymes and regulation.
- Water- and fat-soluble vitamins.
- Nucleotides, DNA/chromatin and replication.
- DNA repair, mutation, transcription, translation and gene regulation.
- Lippincott Chapters 23-28: insulin and glucagon, hypoglycemia, fed-fast integration, diabetes mellitus, obesity, nutrition and vitamins.
- The complete confirmed textbook scaffold: Chapters 1-7, 14-18 and 23-33, with Chapters 8-13 and 19-22 still excluded unless separately confirmed.
- Practical-derived theory: equipment, safety, titration, carbohydrate and amino-acid/protein tests, enzyme assays, spectrophotometry, DNA extraction, chromatography, flame photometry and osmosis.

The standalone `carbohydrate metabolism MD Kish.pdf` remains excluded because Chapters 8-13 were not confirmed. Integrated pathway consequences that are explicitly taught in Chapters 23-27 are included.

### Active Physiology scope

- Homeostasis, body-fluid compartments and cell physiology.
- Membrane structure, diffusion, osmosis and active transport.
- Resting membrane potential and action potentials.

Muscle physiology, neuromuscular transmission and general neurotransmission are assigned to July 25. The later cardiovascular, ECG, circulation and blood Physiology set remains excluded.

Cellular and introductory Histology now appear in the July 29 default as a mandatory safety block. Coverage includes methods, stains, microscopy, membrane, transport, signaling, organelles, cytoskeleton, inclusions, nucleus, cell cycle, division and cell death. The same stable question IDs remain available in July 25 where applicable.
