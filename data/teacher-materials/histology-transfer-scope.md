# Aug 22 unfamiliar-field transfer lab

This bank supplements the teacher’s 15-slide list with unfamiliar normal
micrographs. It is a written-identification bank: no option list is shown before
submission. Each repair compares the correct pattern with the three closest
exam look-alikes.

## Joint terminology that the bank must not collapse

- Structural classes: fibrous, cartilaginous, synovial.
- Functional classes: synarthrosis (essentially immovable), amphiarthrosis
  (slightly movable), diarthrosis (freely movable).
- A typical synovial joint is also a diarthrosis. `Diarthrosis` is not a fourth
  tissue beside synovial tissue.
- A suture is a common fibrous synarthrosis; a syndesmosis is fibrous and can
  permit limited motion; a synchondrosis is a primary cartilaginous joint; a
  symphysis is a secondary cartilaginous joint and usually an amphiarthrosis.

## Tested visual differentials

1. Synovial joint versus cartilaginous symphysis; articular cartilage versus
   the whole joint.
2. Hyaline versus elastic cartilage versus fibrocartilage.
   `Fibroelastic cartilage` is not one of the standard three cartilage classes;
   the intended third class is fibrocartilage.
3. Compact/cortical lamellar bone versus spongy/cancellous architecture versus
   woven/immature matrix. “Spongy” describes architecture; “woven” describes
   matrix maturity, so they are not opposites.
4. Sensory dorsal-root ganglion versus autonomic ganglion versus peripheral
   nerve versus white adipose tissue.
5. Tendon versus ligament versus fibrocartilage versus nerve.
6. Cardiac versus skeletal muscle in longitudinal and transverse fields.
7. White versus brown adipose tissue.
8. Thin hair-bearing versus thick glabrous skin.
9. Simple cuboidal epithelium across renal tubules, thyroid follicles and small
   duct-type fields.
10. Urothelium across relaxed/distended bladder and ureter.
11. Pseudostratified columnar epithelium in respiratory fields (cilia and goblet
    cells) versus epididymis (stereocilia, sperm-filled ducts).

## Image and source rules

- Internet fields come from Wikimedia Commons files whose description pages
  report Public Domain, CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA licensing. Per-file author,
  license, description-page URL and SHA-256 are kept in the generated catalog.
- Commons category membership and filenames are discovery aids, not answer
  keys. A field is included only after independent morphology review.
- Answer-bearing diagrams, labels, captions embedded in the image, pathology
  masquerading as normal tissue, and ambiguous decorative microscopy are
  excluded.
- The two brown-adipose supplements are already-local textbook extractions
  because the clean permissively licensed Commons pool is unusually sparse for
  normal brown fat.
