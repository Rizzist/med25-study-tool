Last updated: 2026-08-18 (Asia/Tehran)

This directory is the provenance boundary for the teacher uploads used to define exam scope. Telegram was accessed in strict read-only mode: files and visible course context were inspected, but no messages, reactions, uploads, group settings, memberships, or unrelated chats were touched.

## Source policy

Teacher files define inclusion and emphasis. Junqueira, Langman, Guyton, Lippincott, and Marks are used to verify mechanisms and explanations—not to silently broaden the syllabus. Every teacher-specific question records the source filename and exact slide, page, or video timestamp when available.

## Deferred media

The inventory records large videos that were not downloaded on airplane Wi-Fi, including duplicate physiology explainers and the large CVS/RS histology and embryology videos. They remain a later enrichment layer; their absence does not block the current teacher-deck bank.
