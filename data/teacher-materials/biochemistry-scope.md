## Bottom line

The local material supports three biochemistry teaching blocks:

1. **Biochemical foundations** - biomolecule classes, central dogma, anabolism/catabolism, ATP, functional groups, and noncovalent/covalent interactions.
2. **Water, acids, bases, and buffers** - body-fluid compartments, water chemistry, pH/pOH, strong versus weak acids, Ka/pKa, titration curves, Henderson-Hasselbalch calculations, and physiological buffer systems.
3. **Vitamins** - water- and fat-soluble vitamins, coenzyme roles, metabolic pathways, deficiency/toxicity, absorption, and clinical associations.

The three teacher-authored course files are `INTRODUCTION.ppt`, `Water and Buffer 1404.ppt`, and `Vitamin 2024.pdf`. The eight-page water/buffer reference and the Lippincott textbook are cross-check sources, not separate syllabus blocks.
