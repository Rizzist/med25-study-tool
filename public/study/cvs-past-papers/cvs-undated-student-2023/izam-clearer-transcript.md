### Q1 · source page 2

The ... have the largest total cross-sectional area of the circulation.

A. capillaries
B. aorta
C. veins
D. arteries

**Answer: A.** The vast number of parallel capillaries gives them the largest aggregate cross-sectional area.


### Q2 · source page 2

Which vessel has greatest blood velocity?

A. Aorta
B. Vena cava
C. Arterioles
D. Capillaries

**Answer: A.** For a given cardiac output, mean velocity is greatest where total cross-sectional area is smallest: the aorta.


### Q3 · source page 2

How would a decrease in vascular resistance affect blood flow?
