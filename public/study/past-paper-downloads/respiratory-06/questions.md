Status: Ungraded - Unresolved: the stem conflates alveolar hypoxia, pulmonary vasoconstriction and arterial hypoxemia without naming the response or defining hypoxia. C. ATP
D. VIP

Source: Original Q17; source page 5.

Status: Ungraded - VIP is the likely teaching distractor, but an absolute exclusion is not adequately established. Primary human histology found VIP immunoreactivity in glomic chief cells (PMID 1700931); primary human experiments directly demonstrate ACh and ATP release (PMID 24887113). Presence is not proof of release, but without specified species/conditions or a source key, the categorical NOT claim is held unresolved.
