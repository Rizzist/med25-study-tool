### 1 · respiratory-19-q001

A 25-year-old man, height 175 cm, has the shown spirogram; temperature coefficient = 1.1. Which one is the following patient’s Tidal Volume?

A. 330 ml
B. 300 ml
C. 270 ml
D. 240 ml

Source: Original Q1; source page 2.

Original figure: /study/respiratory/past-papers/figures/respiratory-19-spirogram.png

Status: Ungraded - Disputed: the source highlights A (330 mL), but the displayed tidal swings run from 1000 to 1400 mL. Applying the stated 1.1 correction gives 440 mL, which is absent from the options. Do not score.
