### 17 · limbs-upper-axial-fragment-q017

Key: A — Posterior longitudinal ligament

Accepted choices for the original wording: A, B, D.

Existing answer note: The posterior longitudinal ligament lies inside the vertebral canal. Its superior continuation, the tectorial membrane, is also internal; the apical ligament of the dens lies in the craniovertebral canal anterior to the tectorial membrane. A, B and D are accepted for this broad wording. The anterior longitudinal ligament is outside, anterior to the bodies.
