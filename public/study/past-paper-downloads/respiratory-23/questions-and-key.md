# Respiratory physiology MCQ reference collection

Undated 72-item physiology teaching bank with five options per item and no supplied answer key. Kept outside default exam-paper selection. Source variants and defective stems are retained; validated keys are editorial checks, not official exam answers.

Source wording, abbreviations and annotated marks are retained. Study keys are independently reviewed or inferred, not authenticated official university answers. Defective or unreadable items are explicitly ungraded; none is silently assigned a default answer.
