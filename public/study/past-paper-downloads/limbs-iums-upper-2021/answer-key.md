### 1 · limbs-iums-upper-2021-q001

Key: A — Injury to the radial nerve causes numbness in the dorsal skin of the lateral 3.5 fingers.

Existing answer note: A is the intended supplied answer, but its sensory territory excludes the distal dorsal fingertips/nail beds, which are largely median supplied. The radial nerve supplies proximal dorsal lateral digits. The anconeus branch accompanies the medial-head branch; posterior forearm cutaneous branches arise in the groove, while the brachialis branch is distal to it.
