### 5 · limbs-lower-axial-fragment-q005

Which of the following muscles is not innervated by a branch of the lumbar sacral trunk?

A. Tensor fasciae latae
B. Biceps femoris
C. Sartorius
D. Popliteus

Practice correction (original choices above): Sartorius is femoral nerve, L2-L3, from the lumbar plexus. The other choices are supplied through superior gluteal or sciatic/tibial pathways. The original calls these 'branches of the lumbar sacral trunk', although named peripheral nerves arise from the plexus, not directly from that trunk.

Source: lower limb · original Qunnumbered (page 5), page 5.
