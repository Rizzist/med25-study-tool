### 13 · respiratory-12-q013

### 36 · respiratory-12-q036

Which statement about the histological structure of the trachea is correct?

A. All epithelial cells have basophilic granules
B. C-shaped cartilage is covered by periosteum
C. Seromucous glands in the lamina propria produce mucus
D. Hyaline C-shaped cartilage is the outermost layer

Source: Original Q36; source page 7.

Status: Ungraded - The supplied circle is C, but the usual layer-specific description places tracheal seromucous glands in the submucosa. The lamina-propria/submucosa boundary is not consistently separated in all descriptions, so C cannot be silently corrected. A is false, cartilage has perichondrium rather than periosteum, and adventitia is external to cartilage. Held ungraded pending clarification of the intended layer convention.
