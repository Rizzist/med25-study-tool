### 11 · divine-ethics-2021-report-selection-q011

Key: C — The modest.

Key provenance: Editorial study answer. Original mark: not established.

Existing answer note: The intended trait is chastity/self-restraint (iffah), translated here as modesty, not shyness. Nahjul Balagha saying 474 compares the reward of one who can sin but remains chaste with the martyr. Its wording is “not greater than”, so the exam’s “more” is stronger than the cited saying; C remains the identifiable intended option.
