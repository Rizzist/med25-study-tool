Preserved as a reference collection outside default-paper selection. Filled student radio buttons are unofficial supplied selections. The 28 screenshot MCQs reuse the July 2022 canonical question IDs, so combined sessions do not repeat them. Four original short-answer prompts are preserved in downloads.

Source wording, abbreviations and annotated marks are retained. Study keys are independently reviewed or inferred, not authenticated official university answers. Defective or unreadable items are explicitly ungraded; none is silently assigned a default answer.
