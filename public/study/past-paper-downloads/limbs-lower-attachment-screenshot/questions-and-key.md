### 12 · limbs-lower-attachment-screenshot-q012

All of the following muscles attach to the ischial tuberosity, EXCEPT:

A. Semitendinosus
B. Adductor magnus
C. Gemellus superior
D. Semimembranosus

Source: lower limb · original Q12, page 1.

## Answer key and review locations

### 12 · limbs-lower-attachment-screenshot-q012

Key: C — Gemellus superior

Existing answer note: Superior gemellus originates from the ischial spine, not the ischial tuberosity. Semitendinosus and semimembranosus originate from the tuberosity, as does the hamstring portion of adductor magnus. Inferior gemellus, unlike superior gemellus, also has an ischial-tuberosity origin. The screenshot's blue selection is not treated as an authenticated source key; this study answer was checked against anatomical references.
