### 10 · divine-ethics-photo-20-additional-selection-q010

Key: B — Any faithful person is a Muslim, but not vice versa.

Existing answer note: Within this course’s technical distinction, inward iman is more than outward profession of Islam; Quran 49:14 illustrates the distinction. This is not permission to pronounce judgment on an individual’s sincerity or to deny moral worth to other people.

### 17 · divine-ethics-photo-20-additional-selection-q017

Key: B — It is not jealousy and is not bad.

Key provenance: Editorial study answer. Original mark: B.

Existing answer note: The intended distinction is constructive emulation without wishing the other person to lose the good quality, versus destructive envy. The stem states no wish to deprive the friend. Compare the review’s envy obstacle; this is not the same as coveting another’s loss.
