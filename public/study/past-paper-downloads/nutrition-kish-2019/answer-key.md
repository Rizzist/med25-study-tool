### 3 · kish-2019-q003

Key: D — Dietary fiber

Key provenance: Source mark with editorial review; not an official university key.

Existing answer note: D is the intended dietary inhibitor among these choices. Phytate and other compounds in some fiber-rich foods inhibit nonheme-iron absorption; it is not true that every purified fiber has this effect. Vitamin C and gastric acidity favor absorption.

Source: Original Q3; page 1.

DeBruyne, Pinna & Whitney, Nutrition & Diet Therapy, 9th ed. (2016), PDF pp. 261–265.
https://ods.od.nih.gov/factsheets/Iron-HealthProfessional/

### 4 · kish-2019-q004

Key: B — Increasing the clotting/Increasing the chemotaxy of neutrophils

Key provenance: Source mark with editorial review; not an official university key.

Existing answer note: Thromboxane A2 promotes platelet aggregation; leukotriene B4 is a neutrophil chemoattractant. Different leukotrienes also have other actions, so LT here refers principally to LTB4.

Source: Original Q4; page 1.

DeBruyne, Pinna & Whitney, Nutrition & Diet Therapy, 9th ed. (2016), PDF pp. 88–89.

Key provenance: Source mark with editorial review; not an official university key.

### 20 · kish-2019-q020

Key: A — Iron

Key provenance: Editorial study answer / source review note; not an official university key.

Existing answer note: Unnecessary iron in an iron-replete person can cause harm; iron overload promotes tissue injury. Cancer or cardiovascular disease alone is not a blanket reason to withhold medically indicated iron. The study wording specifies excess iron without deficiency.

Source: Original Q20; page 3.

DeBruyne, Pinna & Whitney, Nutrition & Diet Therapy, 9th ed. (2016), PDF pp. 263–265.

Key provenance: Editorial study answer / source review note; not an official university key.
