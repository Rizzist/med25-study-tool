### 7 · divine-ethics-1-table-fragment-q007

Key: C — Piety returns the human soul to its original purity and honesty.

Key provenance: Editorial study answer. Original mark: D. Accepted choices: B, C.

Existing answer note: The green source mark is D, but first-term Lesson 3, question 4 explicitly says that linking piety with knowledge does NOT mean ungodly people cannot learn. Thus A is false within these notes and D cannot stand. C directly reproduces the stated explanation. B is also compatible with the lesson's account of discernment, so both B and C are accepted for this imprecise stem. This correction is an editorial study judgment, not an official replacement key.
